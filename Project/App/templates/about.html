About Us

Western Mindanao State University (WMSU) is a premier institution of higher learning in the southern Philippines, dedicated to providing quality education, research, and extension services. With a strong commitment to academic excellence and community development, WMSU offers a wide range of undergraduate and graduate programs designed to equip students with the skills and knowledge needed for national and global competitiveness.

Frequently Ask Questions

How to Apply

Visit the Scholarship Office: Go to the WMSU Scholarship Office in person to obtain the necessary application forms and information.​
Complete the Application Form: Fill out the scholarship application form accurately and legibly.​
Prepare Required Documents: Gather all necessary documents, which may include:
Certified true copy of grades or transcript of records
Certificate of Good Moral Character
Proof of Filipino citizenship (e.g., birth certificate)
Barangay clearance
Proof of family income (e.g., Income Tax Return, Certificate of Indigency)
Two 2×2 ID pictures​
Submit Your Application: Submit the completed application form along with all required documents to the Scholarship Office.

What are the Requirements

Filipino Citizenship: Applicants must be Filipino citizens.​
Academic Standing: A minimum General Weighted Average (GWA) of 93% is often required.​
Non-recipients of Other Scholarships: Applicants should not be beneficiaries of other government scholarships or financial assistance programs.
​Financial Need: Family income should not exceed a specified amount, typically around ₱300,000 annually.

Who are Qualified to Apply

Enrolled or planning to enroll at WMSU – Applicants must be incoming or currently enrolled students of the university.
Financially disadvantaged – Some scholarships prioritize students from low-income families and may require proof of income or a certificate of indigency.
Good moral character – Applicants must not have any disciplinary records and must submit a Certificate of Good Moral Character.
Specific background or field of study – Some scholarships are for specific courses (e.g., agriculture, engineering) or student groups (e.g., children of farmers or barangay officials).
