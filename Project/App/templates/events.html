Western Mindanao State University is pleased to announce the opening of applications for its scholarship programs for the upcoming academic year. These scholarships are offered to support deserving students by providing financial assistance, recognizing academic excellence, and promoting inclusive education. Whether you are a high-achieving student, a member of a marginalized community, or someone in need of financial support, WMSU’s scholarship offerings aim to help you pursue your educational goals and build a brighter future. We encourage all qualified applicants to take this opportunity and apply within the given period.
